About VetLearn

🎯 Purpose

VetLearn is a veterinary clinical case simulator designed for veterinary students, doctors, and paravets to practice diagnosis and treatment in a safe, interactive environment.

📊 Scoring System

Diagnosis Accuracy — 40 pts
Correct 1st try: 40 | With hint: 20

Drug Selection — 40 pts
All correct: 40 | Partial: 20

Dose Accuracy — 20 pts
Correct: 20 | Off by one step: 10

Total per case: 100 pts

🐾 Cases

20 clinical cases covering dogs, cattle, goats, and poultry — including infectious diseases, metabolic conditions, and preventive medicine.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Educational use only. Always consult qualified veterinary professionals for actual clinical decisions.
