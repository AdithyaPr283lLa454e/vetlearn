About VetLearn

🎯 Purpose

VetLearn v2.0 is a veterinary clinical case simulator for vet students, doctors, and paravets. Practice diagnosis and treatment with 25 clinically accurate cases covering dogs, cattle, goats, and poultry.

💊 Medicine Cards

Tap any medicine in the treatment screen to see full drug information — class, dose, routes, species, contraindications, and a clinical pro tip.

🔬 Lab Tests

In the history screen, request lab tests to narrow your diagnosis — just like a real clinic!

📊 Scoring

Diagnosis: 40 pts | Drug Selection: 40 pts | Dose Accuracy: 20 pts | Total: 100 pts per case
